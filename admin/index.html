Yi Xing Studio

Campaigns, products and stories

Sign in to change the home page banner, upload product images and videos, publish an e-brochure or write an editorial story. Every save goes live on the website in about a minute.

Sign in to the editor

The editor is Pages CMS. The owner signs in with GitHub; staff sign in with the email invitation the owner sends them.

First time

  1. Owner: choose Sign in to the editor, then sign in with the yixinggoldsmith GitHub account.
  2. When asked, install Pages CMS on the repository yixinggoldsmith.github.io only, and open it.
  3. To give staff access, open Settings → Collaborators in the editor and invite them by email. They do not need a GitHub account.

What you can change

Campaigns

The home page for a season, such as Christmas or Chinese New Year:

  • announcement bar messages
  • the banner video or image, headline and button
  • the product carousel
  • three promo tiles
  • the editorial feature

Tick Live on the site and set the dates. The live campaign whose dates include today is shown.

Products

Name, reference, collection, description, weight and up to eight images plus a video. The first image appears on cards; the rest show in the product window.

e-Brochures

Add a cover and the pages in order. Design the brochure as usual, export each page as a JPG, then upload them. You can link a page to a product so it gets a View button.

Editorial

Stories built from blocks: heading, text, image, quote, products, buttons. Put a word between *asterisks* in a title to set it in italic gold.

Collections

The collection names and the square tiles under Shop by collection.

Home page

The banner shown when no campaign is live, Book an appointment, the four services and which designed brochures appear.
