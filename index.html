About us

We weigh every piece in front of you.

Yi Xing is a goldsmith and jeweller from Malaysia. We make bracelets from our own alphabet of 916 gold beads and pipes, and sell them by weight and purity, with both printed on the receipt.

Hover or tap the mark to see its two halves.

Our mark

Two strokes of one circle

Gold for the metal we work. Teal for the people we make it for. Neither half closes the circle alone.

The same two colours dress every Yi Xing box: teal outside, and our mark in gold foil inside the lid.

How we work

Three promises at the counter

Weighed in front of you

Gold is priced by weight at the day's rate. Your receipt lists the weight in grams, the purity and the workmanship fee, line by line.

Hallmarked and tested

Every bead and pipe is 916 gold, and every batch of alloy is tested by X-ray fluorescence before it reaches the counter.

Looked after for life

Bring any Yi Xing bracelet back for cleaning, restringing or sizing. We keep a record of every piece we sell.

From sketch to gold

How a bead becomes a bracelet

  1. 01

    Sketch

    Every bead and pipe starts as a drawing, with its cut worked out facet by facet.

  2. 02

    Model

    We model each part to a fraction of a millimetre, so beads, pipes and spacers sit flush once strung.

  3. 03

    Finish

    Made in 916 gold, then cut, polished and strung into the finished design.

Questions

Frequently asked

How is the price of a bracelet worked out?

Our bracelets are priced by weight: the weight in grams times the day's gold rate, plus a workmanship fee for the design. Your receipt shows each part.

What do 1C, 2C and 3C mean?

They are our colour codes. 1C is all yellow gold, 2C is two-tone with white gold, and 3C is tri-tone with white and rose gold. All three are 916 gold.

Can you resize a bracelet?

Yes. We add or remove beads and pipes to fit your wrist, usually while you wait, at no charge.

Can I trade in old gold?

Yes, at any boutique. We weigh and test it in front of you and quote at the day's buy-back rate. You can take the value in cash or put it towards a new piece.

Can I reserve a design and collect it at another boutique?

Yes. Ask a client advisor to reserve it, and we will move it to the boutique you choose, in Malaysia or Singapore, within three working days.
